# Phase 12 - Mo rong sau MVP

## Muc tieu

Sau khi MVP on dinh, mo rong san pham thanh nen tang hoc tieng Anh lau dai.

## Huong mo rong san pham

- Thi thu TOEIC full test
- AI giai thich cau sai
- Goi y lo trinh theo muc tieu diem
- Spaced repetition nang cao
- Subscription tra phi
- Mobile app React Native
- Speaking practice
- IELTS hoac giao tiep
- Business English
- Leaderboard
- Reminder hoc moi ngay

## Huong mo rong ky thuat

- Tach worker xu ly nen
- Queue cho email va job cham diem nang
- Cache voi Redis
- Object storage Cloudflare R2
- Observability: logs, metrics, tracing
- Rate limit API
- Audit log cho admin
- Multi-tenant neu ban cho trung tam tieng Anh

## Uu tien nen lam sau MVP

1. Thi thu TOEIC full test
2. AI giai thich cau sai
3. Goi y lo trinh hoc theo diem muc tieu
4. Thanh toan khoa hoc
5. Audio storage production tot hon

## Checklist hoan thanh

- [ ] Co nguoi dung that dung MVP
- [ ] Co feedback ve noi dung hoc
- [ ] Biet tinh nang nao duoc dung nhieu
- [ ] Biet pain point lon nhat cua nguoi hoc
- [ ] Chon mot huong mo rong co tac dong ro rang

## Ket qua dau ra

Ban co lo trinh phat trien tiep dua tren du lieu va feedback, khong mo rong theo cam tinh.
